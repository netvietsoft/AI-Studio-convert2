======================================================================
13. P1 ALGORITHM CANDIDATES
======================================================================

Architect/Agent shall evaluate at least:

A. Structure Tensor
- image gradients Ix, Iy;
- tensor J = [[Ix², IxIy], [IxIy, Iy²]];
- Gaussian/local smoothing;
- principal eigenvector;
- hair tangent perpendicular/consistent with strand structure.

B. Oriented Filter Bank / Gabor
- response at multiple angles;
- choose dominant orientation;
- confidence from peak separation.

C. Multi-scale Hessian / ridge direction
for fine strand/ridge patterns.

D. Hybrid:
Structure Tensor primary
+ Gabor fallback in low-confidence regions
+ spatial coherence regularization.

Do not claim best algorithm before benchmark.

======================================================================
15. P1 ANGLE SEMANTICS
======================================================================

Hair orientation is PI-periodic:

theta and theta + pi represent same strand direction.

Do not interpolate raw angle linearly across wrap boundaries.

Recommended representation:

vx = cos(2*theta)
vy = sin(2*theta)

interpolate/regularize doubled-angle vector,
then recover:

theta = 0.5 * atan2(vy, vx)

======================================================================
16. P1 CONFIDENCE
======================================================================

- strong directional strand region;

- highlight saturation.

Possible structure tensor coherence:

C = (lambda1 - lambda2) / (lambda1 + lambda2 + eps)

Freeze formula only after experiments.
